# X0X UI v2

The bar: Baud Girl's FM-1+VA (one navigation grammar everywhere, the four knobs always live,
every change answered on screen, confirmation before anything is lost, real units, themes).
X0X is a groovebox, not a synth, so the grammar is its own: the step grid is the home of
every part, and switching part and pattern is one turn away at all times.

## Rules

1. **Two knobs move, four knobs play.** ALGORITHM picks the part, PRESETS cues the pattern,
   SELECT moves (page, list row), KNOB 1-4 change what the knob strip shows. That holds on
   every screen; the knob strip never disappears.
2. **SEL opens, HOME goes back.** SEL (printed SEL; Felucca's "SCL") opens the list of
   everything on the current screen, runs a list's action, answers a question with yes.
   HOME closes whatever is open, else goes home.
3. **Every change is answered.** Turning a knob lifts its cell and shows the parameter large
   across the screen for about a second. Button actions say what they did in the header.
4. **Nothing is lost without a question.** Clearing a part, copying over a pattern that has
   notes, a factory reset: a question across the screen, SEL yes, HOME no.
5. **You always know where you are.** The header: the part (in its colour), the screen and
   its page dots, the pattern (and the cued one), the tempo, transport, and a dot while
   anything is unsaved.
6. **Colour means part.** 909 orange, 808 red, 303 A green, 303 B cyan, BREAK violet:
   chip, grid, meters, knob arcs. The theme (GLO) colours the chrome only.
7. **Units where they mean something** (dB, ms, %, note names, divisions); the drum
   machines' pots stay 0-127, as their panels.

## Screen

```
 0  header (20)      [PART chip] SCREEN ....  P03>05  128  > o  .
20  main (144)       the screen's view; lists and questions draw here
164 knob strip (76)  4 cells: name, arc, value (+ unit)
```

## Screens

| Button | Screen | Main area | Knob pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOME | HOME | patterns, the five parts (steps, mute, level) | PERFORM (tempo, swing, pump, cutoff), LEVELS |
| ALGORITHM / EDIT | PART | 909/808 grid, 303 line, break waveform + slices | the voice / part, SENDS, PART |
| ARP | TB-3PO | the line + generator state | generator, scale, line |
| FX | FX | sends (5 parts x reverb, delay), delay and reverb | SENDS, REVERB, DELAY, KIT DRIVE |
| LFO | MIX | channel strips, master out and gain reduction | LEVELS, COMP, COMP 2, FILTER |
| GLO | GLOBAL | a list: MIDI, keys, theme, save, clear, about | PERFORM |

Hold HOME and turn SELECT: tempo, anywhere (PLAY acts on the press, for timing).
