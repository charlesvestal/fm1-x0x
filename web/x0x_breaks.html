Break loops

X0X has three slots for your own breaks, about 7 seconds each. A slot can hold one loop or several. On the FM-1 they appear on the BREAK part's EDIT page, as choices for LOOP A and LOOP B.

Connect the FM-1 by USB with X0X running, and use Chrome or Edge.

Loops follow the tempo by playing faster or slower, so their pitch follows it too. The break's PITCH knob shifts it. The manual's BREAK section says how X0X rearranges a loop as it plays.

From the command line: python3 tools/upload_breaks.py --bars amen.wav think.wav …. X0X is GPL-3.0, built on Felucca by Hügelton Instruments; the break generator is BB Gen's. github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-x0x
