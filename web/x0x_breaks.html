Break loops for X0X

Load your own breaks into X0X's BREAK part, straight from the browser. Connect the FM-1 by USB with X0X running, add some loops, and press Upload. Use Chrome or Edge.

Each loop becomes a choice for LOOP A and LOOP B on the BREAK part's LOOPS page, named BR1.1, BR1.2 and so on, after X0X's own two loops. The FM-1 has three slots of about 7 seconds each, so about nine one-bar loops fit. Uploading to a slot replaces what was in it.
