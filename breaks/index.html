X0X

Break loops

Your own loops for X0X's BREAK part. They appear on the FM-1 as BR1, BR2 and so on, after X0X's own loops, when you turn LOOP A or LOOP B (BREAK, then EDIT).

Connect the FM-1 by USB with X0X running, and use Chrome or Edge.

Connect to see the loops on the FM-1.
or drop files here. Drop one on a loop to replace it.

Loops follow the tempo by playing faster or slower, so their pitch follows it too; the break's PITCH knob shifts it. Removing a loop renumbers the ones after it, so patterns that used them play the next loop instead. The manual's BREAK section says how X0X plays a loop.
