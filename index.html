X0X

X0X is groovebox firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. It plays a 909, an 808, two 303s and a breakbeat player, all at once, and TB-3PO writes 303 lines for you. Write patterns, arrange them into a song and record your knob moves.

X0X installs and uninstalls the way Felucca does, and the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. You install it at your own risk.
Try it in the browserListen right here, and play it with a mouse, a touch screen or the keyboard. It runs the same code as the FM-1, so you don't need one.
Install v1.2.1 What's new?Connect your FM-1 by USB and install from Chrome or Edge. There's nothing to install on your computer.
